Most luxury launches in the NCR ask you to imagine the neighbourhood that will exist in five years. Legacy by Gaurs asks you to look out of the window at one that already does.

The project sits at Plot B-10 inside Jaypee Greens — a golf township that has been delivered, occupied and operating for years, wrapped around an 18-hole course designed by Greg Norman. What Gaurs Group has added is the piece that was missing: an ultra-luxury residential landmark at its centre.

This guide is for buyers who are serious. Whether you are upgrading from an apartment, buying a second home, or evaluating a genuinely finite asset as an investor, everything the brochure discloses is set out below — and everything it does not disclose is flagged as such.

## About Legacy by Gaurs

<a id="about"></a>

Legacy by Gaurs is an ultra-luxury development by <a href="https://www.gaursonsindia.com" className="text-blue-600 underline">Gaurs Group</a>, built inside Jaypee Greens at Sectors 19 and 25, Greater Noida. It comprises **265 four-bedroom condominiums** across four towers and **20 Legacy Villas**.

The defining characteristic is scarcity of a kind that is unusual in the NCR. There is no studio inventory, no 2 BHK, no 3 BHK. Every single condominium in the project is a 4 BHK. The smallest is 3,510 sq. ft. and the largest is 4,765 sq. ft. There are only twenty villas, ever.

The project's identity is built around the **Fleur** — the fleur-de-lis, a French emblem of nobility and heritage. It runs through the branding, the architecture and the naming: the towers are Buckingham, Edinburgh, Kensington and Versailles; the clubhouse is the House of Royals; the sky bridge is the HELIX; the podium is Windsor.

<a id="towers"></a>

Each tower is built to the same structural specification — **3 basements + ground/stilt + 34 floors** — and each carries two of the project's eight unit plans.

## The Legacy Villas

<a id="villas"></a>

There are exactly **20 Legacy Villas**, numbered LV01 through LV21 (there is no LV13). They sit in a cluster between the golf course and Pari Chowk, along a 12-metre right-of-way road.

Fourteen villas are built over a **double basement** — LV01, LV03 through LV12, and LV14 through LV16. The remaining six sit over a **single basement**: LV02, LV17, LV18, LV19, LV20 and LV21.

Each villa rises through a ground floor, a mezzanine, a first floor, a second floor and a terrace level, above its basement or basements. The brochure publishes plans for every one of these levels.

<Callout type="warning">
  Twenty villas is a genuinely finite number. If a villa is your requirement rather than a preference, availability — not price — is likely to be the binding constraint.
</Callout>

## Pricing

**Gaurs Group has not published a public price list for Legacy by Gaurs.** The brochure carries floor plans, areas, specifications, RERA details and collection account details — but no rate card.

This is normal for a project at this end of the market, where pricing is released per unit and moves with the floor, the tower, and whether the residence faces the golf course or Pari Chowk. ## Amenities & Lifestyle

<a id="amenities"></a>

The amenity programme is stacked across three levels — and this is the part of the project that most clearly justifies the "ultra-luxury" label.

### House of Royals — the clubhouse

Also called Club Imperial, this is the social heart of the project. The brochure lists a café, restaurant, library, meeting room, conference room, lobby lounge, theatre, party hall, billiards lounge, cards room, gym and yoga, game zone, multipurpose hall, music/hobby room, healthcare, a spa-sauna wellness and treatment zone, a swimming pool with kids' pool and jacuzzi, a pool-side outdoor seating deck, and a separate **indoor heated pool**.

### HELIX — the sky bridge

Suspended between the towers: tree court, pavilion, waterbody, lawn, parterre garden, yoga and meditation deck, seating with trellis, open-air theatre, chess table, jogging track, celebration court, snooker table, herbal garden, bar counter, table tennis, carrom and foosball.

### Windsor — the landscaped podium

At the base: tropical garden, half basketball court, ball pit, bouncing net, discovering net, water features, flower garden, badminton court, yoga deck, outdoor gym, moss garden, kids' gym and play area, and sit-out spaces.

### And the township beyond

Because Legacy sits inside Jaypee Greens, residents also inherit the township's own infrastructure: the **Atlantic** sports complex, the **Boomerang Club**, a golf and spa resort, the Town Centre, a public school, and of course the 18-hole Greg Norman golf course — one of the longest on the global circuit.

## What to Verify Before You Buy

<a id="diligence"></a>

This is a marketing site operated by an authorised channel partner, not by the developer. Treat the following as your own checklist, not ours.

<Callout type="warning">
