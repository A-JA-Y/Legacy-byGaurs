  The Greater Noida Authority approved a 3.58% revision in circle rates during its 143rd board meeting on May 3, 2026 — the first such revision in nearly nine years. For anyone currently evaluating a purchase in the corridor, including at Legacy by Gaurs in Jaypee Greens, it is worth understanding precisely what this does and does not change.

  The revision, aligned with the Cost Inflation Index, is a long-overdue correction that narrows the gap between government-notified rates and actual market prices across Greater Noida's premium residential zones.

  ## What a circle rate actually is
  <a id="importance"></a>

  Circle rates are the minimum benchmark valuations set by the government for property registration. Stamp duty and registration charges — currently **7% in Uttar Pradesh** (5% stamp duty plus 2% registration) — are levied on the *higher* of the circle rate or the actual transaction value.

  This is the key point, and it is routinely misreported: **for a premium project transacting well above circle rate, a circle rate hike has almost no direct cost impact on the buyer.** Your duty was already being calculated on the transaction price, because the transaction price was the higher figure. Raising the floor does not raise your bill unless the floor moves above your price — which, in the luxury segment, it does not.

  So the revision matters as a **signal**, not as a cost. It is the state formally acknowledging that values in this corridor have moved — after nine years of pretending otherwise.

  ## Where Legacy by Gaurs sits in this
  <a id="project-glance"></a>

  Legacy by Gaurs is a Gaurs Group development at Plot B-10, Jaypee Greens, Sectors 19 and 25, Greater Noida — inside an established golf township, overlooking an 18-hole course designed by Greg Norman on one side and Pari Chowk on the other.

  The project comprises **265 four-bedroom condominiums** across four towers — Buckingham, Edinburgh, Kensington and Versailles, each 34 floors — plus **20 Legacy Villas**. Every condominium is a 4 BHK, ranging from 3,510 to 4,765 sq. ft. of total area. It is registered under RERA number UPRERAPRJ688396/10/2024.

  Because Gaurs Group has not published a public price list for this project, no responsible reading of the circle rate revision can tell you what a unit here now "should" cost. What it does tell you is the direction of official sentiment on the corridor.

  ## The infrastructure context
  <a id="infrastructure"></a>

  The circle rate revision arrived alongside a more consequential announcement: the Greater Noida Authority cleared a proposal for a **37-km, 105-metre-wide road** connecting Greater Noida to the Hapur bypass, with onward linkage to the Ganga Expressway. The corridor will pass through Sector Alpha 2, NH-91 and the Eastern Peripheral Expressway, and is estimated for completion within two to three years.

  Set against the **Noida International Airport at Jewar** and the proposed metro extension from Pari Chowk toward Jewar, the infrastructure pipeline around this corridor is the densest it has been.

  A note of realism, though: infrastructure timelines in India slip. Treat the pipeline as a directional positive over a multi-year hold, not as a dated catalyst you can underwrite.

  ## What buyers should actually do
  <a id="next-steps"></a>

  The practical takeaways are narrower than the headlines suggest:

  - **Your registration cost barely moves** if you are buying at market price in the premium segment. Model 7% on your transaction value and you are close enough.
  - **The signal is real.** Circle rate revisions have historically preceded broader re-ratings in infrastructure-rich corridors, because the state only revises upward once the gap has become indefensible.
  - **Supply, not sentiment, determines whether it reaches you.** In a project capped at 265 condominiums and 20 villas with no scope for further phases, a corridor-wide re-rating has nowhere to dissipate. In a corridor absorbing tens of thousands of new units, it does.
  - **Verify before you transact.** Confirm RERA UPRERAPRJ688396/10/2024 at up-rera.in, get the unit-specific price in writing with statutory charges itemised, and remit only into the project's designated collection account.

  One more thing worth flagging, since it affects your all-in number far more than a 3.58% circle rate move: the Legacy by Gaurs brochure states that units are handed over as a **raw structure with façade development**, with works not listed in the specifications falling within the buyer's scope. Budget for fit-out.
